Ketch is a fast, open-source download manager. It splits every download across parallel connections, shows each one as a live lane, and lets you control the downloads on your computers and home server from your phone.

FASTER DOWNLOADS
• Parallel connections: Ketch downloads several ranges of a file at once and shows the speed of each. Add or remove connections while a download runs.
• Several networks at once: spread one download over Wi-Fi and mobile data.
• Pause and resume, even after a restart. Ketch checks that the file on the server has not changed before it continues, and retries failed connections on its own.
• Downloads keep running in the background, with their progress in a notification.

EVERY KIND OF LINK
• HTTP and HTTPS
• FTP and FTPS, with parallel connections and resume
• BitTorrent and magnet links: v1, v2 and hybrid torrents, with a choice of files
• Open magnet links and .torrent files from your browser or file manager, or share a link to Ketch from any app

ALL YOUR DEVICES IN ONE APP
• Pair the Ketch desktop app on a Mac, Windows or Linux computer, or a home server running Ketch, by scanning a QR code
• See every device with its live speed, and add, pause or resume downloads on any of them
• Send a download to another device, or list the downloads of every device in one place

IN CONTROL OF YOUR BANDWIDTH
• Speed modes: Full speed, a Slow lane that leaves room for calls and streaming, or Auto, which switches between them on a weekly schedule
• Speed limits, connection counts and priorities per download, changed while it runs. Urgent starts a download right away.
• A queue that limits how many downloads run at once, and downloads that start at a time you pick

FIND DOWNLOADS WITH AI (PREVIEW)
• Discover: describe what you want, and an AI agent searches the web, checks the links and ranks the files it finds. Narrow it down with follow-up messages.
• Bring your own model: OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Ollama or any OpenAI-compatible service, with your own key. Discover asks before it opens a website, and you can switch it off.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no tracking. Ketch has no servers of its own: it connects only to the sites you download from, the devices you pair and the AI service you choose.
• Open source under the Apache 2.0 license: https://github.com/linroid/Ketch

Light and dark themes, in English, 简体中文, 繁體中文, 日本語, 한국어, Español, Português (Brasil), Deutsch, Français and Русский.

Ketch downloads what you ask it to. Please only download content you have the right to.

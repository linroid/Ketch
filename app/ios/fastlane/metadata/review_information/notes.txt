Ketch is a download manager. It needs no account or login, and every feature works on this device alone.

To try it:
1. Tap Add, paste a link to a large file and tap Download. Any large public file works, for example an asset of https://github.com/linroid/Ketch/releases/latest (copy the link of a .dmg or .msi file).
2. Tap the download to open its details, then the Connections tab to see its parallel connections, each with its own speed. Pause and resume it from the same sheet.
3. Finished files appear in the Files app under On My iPhone › Ketch.
4. Settings › Speed shows the speed modes and their weekly schedule.

Pairing with other devices (Devices › Add device) is optional. It needs a computer running the Ketch desktop app, or a server running "ketch server", on the same network. The Local Network permission is only used to find those devices.

Ketch has no servers of its own and collects no data. It is open source: https://github.com/linroid/Ketch
